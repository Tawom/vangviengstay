VANG VIENG STAY - CANCELLATION & REFUND POLICY

Last Updated: November 2026

This policy applies to all bookings made through the Vang Vieng Stay platform, including guesthouses, tours, local guides, motorbike rentals, and buggy car rentals.

GENERAL CANCELLATION RULES

All cancellations must be made through your Vang Vieng Stay dashboard or by contacting our support team at support@vangviengstay.com. Cancellations made via social media or third-party platforms are not valid.

Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after approval.

GUESTHOUSE CANCELLATIONS

Cancellation more than 7 days before check-in: 100% refund of any deposit paid.
Cancellation 3-7 days before check-in: 50% refund of deposit.
Cancellation less than 3 days before check-in: No refund.
No-show: No refund.

Special dates (Lao New Year, Christmas, New Year's Eve): 14-day advance notice required for any refund.

TOUR CANCELLATIONS

Cancellation more than 48 hours before tour start time: 100% refund.
Cancellation 24-48 hours before tour start time: 50% refund.
Cancellation less than 24 hours before tour start time: No refund.
No-show: No refund.

Weather-related cancellations: If the tour operator cancels due to unsafe weather conditions (heavy rain, flooding, storms), you will receive a 100% refund or the option to reschedule at no extra cost.

LOCAL GUIDE CANCELLATIONS

Cancellation more than 24 hours before booking start time: 100% refund.
Cancellation less than 24 hours before booking start time: No refund.
No-show: No refund.

If your guide cancels for any reason, you will receive a 100% refund or assistance booking an alternative guide.

MOTORBIKE RENTAL CANCELLATIONS

Cancellation more than 24 hours before rental start date: 100% refund of any deposit.
Cancellation less than 24 hours before rental start date: No refund.
No-show: No refund.

Early returns: No refund for unused rental days. The rental period is fixed at booking.

BUGGY CAR RENTAL CANCELLATIONS

Cancellation more than 24 hours before booking date: 100% refund.
Cancellation less than 24 hours before booking date: No refund.
No-show: No refund.

DEPOSIT REFUNDS

Motorbike rentals: Security deposit (cash or passport) is returned immediately upon safe return of the vehicle, minus any charges for:
- Damage beyond normal wear and tear
- Late return (charged at the hourly or daily rate)
- Missing equipment (helmet, lock, documents)
- Fuel not refilled (if applicable)

Guesthouses: Deposit is returned at check-out after room inspection, minus charges for:
- Property damage
- Missing items
- Smoking in non-smoking rooms
- Excessive cleaning required

DISPUTES

If you believe a refund was incorrectly denied, contact support@vangviengstay.com within 48 hours of the cancellation. Our team will review your case within 2 business days.

Vang Vieng Stay acts as a booking platform connecting guests with independent operators. We mediate disputes but are not liable for operator decisions made in accordance with these policies.

EMERGENCY EXCEPTIONS

In cases of documented medical emergencies, natural disasters, or government travel restrictions, Vang Vieng Stay will work with operators to provide full refunds or rescheduling options on a case-by-case basis.

Contact: support@vangviengstay.com
Response time: Within 24 hours
