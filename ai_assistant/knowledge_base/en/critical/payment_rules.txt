VANG VIENG STAY - PAYMENT RULES & DEPOSIT REQUIREMENTS

Last Updated: November 2026

PAYMENT METHODS

Vang Vieng Stay operates on a "book now, pay later" model. You do not pay online when making a reservation. Payment is collected in cash directly with the operator at the time of service.

Accepted payment currencies:
- Lao Kip (LAK) - Primary currency
- US Dollars (USD) - Accepted by most operators at daily exchange rate
- Thai Baht (THB) - Accepted at some locations

Credit cards and online payments are NOT currently supported. We recommend bringing sufficient cash for your bookings.

EXCHANGE RATES

For guests paying in USD or THB, operators use the official daily exchange rate from BCEL (Banque pour le Commerce Exterieur Lao) or LDB (Lao Development Bank). Rates are updated daily and posted at most guesthouses and tour offices.

Approximate reference rates (subject to daily change):
- 1 USD = 20,000 - 21,000 LAK
- 1 THB = 600 - 650 LAK

Always confirm the exchange rate before paying in foreign currency.

DEPOSIT REQUIREMENTS

GUESTHOUSES
Most guesthouses do not require a deposit. Some may request:
- One night's advance payment during peak season (December-February, Lao New Year)
- Passport or ID card as security (returned at check-out)

TOURS
No deposit required. Full payment is made at the tour meeting point or at pickup.

LOCAL GUIDES
No deposit required. Payment is made directly to the guide at the start of the tour.

MOTORBIKE RENTALS
A security deposit is required before taking the motorbike. Options:
- Cash deposit: Typically 2,000,000 - 3,000,000 LAK (varies by motorbike type)
- Original passport: Held as collateral (returned upon safe return)
- Some operators accept a photocopy of passport plus cash deposit

The deposit is fully refunded when you return the motorbike in good condition.

BUGGY CARS
A security deposit is required:
- 2-seat buggy: 1,500,000 - 2,000,000 LAK
- 4-seat buggy: 2,500,000 - 3,500,000 LAK

Deposit is refunded upon safe return of the vehicle.

PAYMENT TIMING

Guesthouses: Payment at check-in
Tours: Payment at meeting point or pickup (usually 30 minutes before departure)
Guides: Payment at the start of the tour
Motorbikes: Payment plus deposit at pickup
Buggy cars: Payment plus deposit at pickup

LATE PAYMENT

If you fail to pay at the agreed time, your booking may be cancelled and your deposit (if any) forfeited. Operators will typically wait 30 minutes past the scheduled time before releasing your booking.

RECEIPTS

Always request a receipt for your payment. Vang Vieng Stay booking confirmations include a reference number (format: VV-XXXX for guesthouses, TV-XXXX for tours, VG-XXXX for guides, MB-XXXX for motorbikes, BG-XXXX for buggies).

Show this reference number to the operator when making payment.

TIPS

Tipping is not required but appreciated for excellent service:
- Guides: 50,000 - 200,000 LAK per day
- Tour drivers: 20,000 - 50,000 LAK per trip
- Guesthouse staff: Not expected

SCAMS & FRAUD PROTECTION

- Never pay anyone claiming to be from Vang Vieng Stay via Western Union, money transfer, or cryptocurrency
- Always pay directly to the operator at the time of service
- Verify operator identity using the reference number from your booking confirmation
- Report suspicious activity to support@vangviengstay.com

PRICE CHANGES

Prices are confirmed at the time of booking. Operators cannot charge more than the listed price unless:
- You request additional services not included in the original booking
- You extend your rental or stay beyond the booked period
- There are additional charges for damages or late returns

Any price changes must be agreed upon in advance and documented.
