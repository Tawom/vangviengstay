VANG VIENG STAY - PRICING INFORMATION

Last Updated: November 2026

All prices on Vang Vieng Stay are listed in Lao Kip (LAK). Payment is made in cash directly to operators at the time of service.

CURRENCY & EXCHANGE

Primary currency: Lao Kip (LAK)
US Dollars (USD) widely accepted
Thai Baht (THB) accepted at many locations

Approximate exchange rates (check daily rates):
- 1 USD = 20,000 - 21,000 LAK
- 1 THB = 600 - 650 LAK
- 1 EUR = 22,000 - 23,000 LAK

ATMs are available in Vang Vieng town (BCEL, LDB, JDB banks) but may have withdrawal limits. Carry sufficient cash for bookings.

GUESTHOUSE PRICING

Prices vary by season and room type:

Low season (May - October):
- Dorm bed: 80,000 - 150,000 LAK per night
- Single room (fan): 150,000 - 250,000 LAK per night
- Single room (AC): 200,000 - 350,000 LAK per night
- Double room (fan): 250,000 - 400,000 LAK per night
- Double room (AC): 350,000 - 600,000 LAK per night

High season (November - April):
Prices typically 20-50% higher than low season.

Peak dates (Lao New Year mid-April, Christmas, New Year):
Prices may be 50-100% higher. Advance booking essential.

What's typically included:
- Clean bedding and towels
- Wi-Fi
- Hot water (most properties)
- Basic toiletries (varies)

What's usually NOT included:
- Breakfast (some guesthouses include it)
- Airport/station transfers
- Activities and tours

TOUR PRICING

Tour prices are per person and typically include:
- Professional guide
- Transportation (depending on tour)
- Required equipment
- Entry fees to attractions
- Water and sometimes snacks

Common tour price ranges:

Half-day tours (3-4 hours):
- Village walking tours: 200,000 - 400,000 LAK per person
- Kayaking tours: 300,000 - 500,000 LAK per person
- Waterfall tours: 250,000 - 450,000 LAK per person

Full-day tours (6-8 hours):
- Rock climbing: 600,000 - 1,000,000 LAK per person
- Zip-lining adventures: 500,000 - 800,000 LAK per person
- Multi-activity tours: 700,000 - 1,200,000 LAK per person
- Cave exploration: 400,000 - 700,000 LAK per person

Multi-day tours:
- 2-day trekking: 1,200,000 - 2,000,000 LAK per person
- 3-day adventure packages: 2,000,000 - 3,500,000 LAK per person

Group discounts:
- 4+ people: Usually 10-15% discount
- 8+ people: Usually 15-20% discount
- Private tours: Higher price but exclusive experience

LOCAL GUIDE PRICING

Guides are priced by duration:

Hourly rate: 150,000 - 300,000 LAK per hour
Half-day (up to 4 hours): 500,000 - 900,000 LAK
Full-day (up to 8 hours): 900,000 - 1,500,000 LAK

What's included:
- Guide's time and expertise
- Local knowledge and translation
- Assistance with logistics

What's NOT included:
- Entry fees to attractions
- Food and drinks
- Transportation (unless specified)
- Equipment rental

Guide expertise affects price:
- Basic local guides: Lower end of range
- Specialized guides (climbing, photography, birdwatching): Higher end
- Multi-lingual guides: May charge premium

PRICE COMPARISON TIPS

To get the best value:
- Book multiple days for motorbikes (significant discounts)
- Book tours with groups (per-person cost drops)
- Travel in low season (May-October) for guesthouse deals
- Book in advance for peak dates (prices increase closer to date)

HIDDEN COSTS TO AWARE OF

- Fuel for motorbikes/buggies
- Entry fees to attractions (sometimes not included in tour price)
- Tips for guides and drivers (optional but appreciated)
- Equipment rental (kayaks, climbing gear, etc.)
- Food and drinks during tours
- Transportation to tour meeting points
- Damage charges for rentals

PRICE GUARANTEE

Vang Vieng Stay guarantees the price shown at booking. Operators cannot charge more than the listed price unless:
- You add extra services
- You extend your rental/stay
- There are damage or late return charges

All price changes must be agreed in advance.

SEASONAL PRICE VARIATIONS

Low season (May - October):
- Guesthouses: 20-40% cheaper
- Tours: Frequent discounts and deals
- Motorbikes/buggies: Lower prices, more availability

High season (November - April):
- Guesthouses: Higher prices, book in advance
- Tours: Peak pricing, limited availability
- Motorbikes/buggies: Standard pricing

Peak periods (Lao New Year, Christmas, New Year):
- All services: 50-100% price increases
- Book 2-4 weeks in advance
- Some operators have minimum stay requirements

PAYMENT CURRENCY TIPS

- Pay in LAK for best rates
- USD accepted but exchange rate may be unfavorable
- Bring small bills for easier transactions
- ATMs dispense LAK only
- Credit cards rarely accepted
