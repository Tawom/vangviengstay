VANG VIENG STAY - LOCAL GUIDE BOOKING TERMS & CONDITIONS

Last Updated: November 2026

Local guides provide authentic cultural experiences and expert knowledge of Vang Vieng. This document covers booking terms, expectations, and guidelines.

WHAT LOCAL GUIDES OFFER

Local guides are independent professionals who provide:
- Cultural knowledge and historical context
- Translation services (Lao to English and other languages)
- Navigation and logistics assistance
- Access to local experiences not available to independent travelers
- Safety guidance for activities
- Photography assistance
- Restaurant and activity recommendations
- Insight into Lao daily life and customs

GUIDE TYPES & SPECIALIZATIONS

General local guides:
- Village tours and cultural experiences
- Town orientation and market visits
- Basic translation assistance
- Restaurant and activity recommendations

Specialized guides:
- Rock climbing guides (certified)
- Trekking and hiking guides
- Photography guides
- Birdwatching guides
- Culinary and cooking guides
- History and archaeology guides
- Adventure activity guides (kayaking, caving)

Multi-lingual guides:
- English (most common)
- French (some guides)
- Chinese (Mandarin)
- Thai
- Vietnamese

Languages vary by guide. Specify your language needs when booking.

BOOKING PROCESS

Step 1: Browse available guides
- View profiles on Vang Vieng Stay
- Check languages, expertise, reviews
- Compare pricing

Step 2: Make booking request
- Select date and time
- Specify duration (hourly, half-day, full-day)
- Provide meeting location
- Add special requests or requirements

Step 3: Receive confirmation
- Guide reviews your request
- Confirmation sent within 24 hours
- You receive guide's contact information

Step 4: Meet your guide
- Meet at agreed location and time
- Confirm itinerary and expectations
- Enjoy your experience!

PRICING STRUCTURE

Hourly rate:
- 150,000 - 300,000 LAK per hour
- Minimum booking: Usually 2 hours
- Best for: Short orientations, specific questions

Half-day (up to 4 hours):
- 500,000 - 900,000 LAK
- Most popular option
- Good for: Village tours, half-day activities

Full-day (up to 8 hours):
- 900,000 - 1,500,000 LAK
- Best value for full-day experiences
- Includes lunch break (not included in price)

What's included in guide fee:
- Guide's time and expertise
- Local knowledge
- Translation services
- Assistance with logistics

What's NOT included:
- Entry fees to attractions
- Food and drinks (for you and guide)
- Transportation costs
- Equipment rental
- Activities (climbing, kayaking, etc.)
- Tips (optional but appreciated)

Additional costs to budget:
- Attraction entry fees: 20,000 - 100,000 LAK per site
- Guide's meals: Expected that you cover guide's food during tour
- Transportation: Tuk-tuk, motorbike taxi, or rental
- Activity costs: Tours, equipment rental, etc.

MEETING & LOGISTICS

Meeting location options:
- Your guesthouse (most convenient)
- Central meeting point in town
- Activity starting location
- Bus station or airport (may have additional fee)

Confirm meeting details:
- Exact location
- Time (guide arrives 5-10 minutes early)
- What to wear/bring
- Itinerary for the day
- Emergency contact numbers

Communication:
- Guides typically speak English
- Provide your WhatsApp or local number if available
- Confirm meeting day before
- Notify guide of any changes promptly

GUIDE RESPONSIBILITIES

Your guide will:
- Arrive on time at meeting location
- Provide professional, friendly service
- Share accurate local knowledge
- Ensure your safety during activities
- Assist with translation and communication
- Help with logistics and planning
- Respect your pace and interests
- Maintain appropriate boundaries

Guide will NOT:
- Provide illegal services
- Encourage unsafe activities
- Pressure you to buy specific products
- Share personal information about other clients
- Work beyond agreed hours without additional payment

YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES

As a client, you agree to:
- Provide accurate contact information
- Meet guide at agreed time and location
- Communicate any changes promptly
- Respect guide's knowledge and time
- Follow safety instructions
- Cover guide's meals during tour
- Pay agreed fee promptly
- Treat guide with respect and courtesy
- Provide honest feedback/review

TIPPING

Tipping is not required but appreciated for excellent service:
- Half-day tour: 50,000 - 100,000 LAK
- Full-day tour: 100,000 - 200,000 LAK
- Specialized guides: Higher amounts appropriate

Tips go directly to guide (not platform).

CANCELLATION POLICY

Cancellation more than 24 hours before booking:
- 100% refund

Cancellation less than 24 hours before booking:
- No refund

No-show:
- No refund

Guide cancellation:
- If guide cancels for any reason: 100% refund or assistance finding replacement

Rescheduling:
- Allowed with 24+ hours notice
- Subject to guide availability

PAYMENT

Payment is made directly to guide:
- At start of tour (most common)
- At end of tour (if agreed in advance)
- Cash only (LAK or USD)
- No online payment through platform

Always request receipt or confirmation of payment.

DURING THE TOUR

Communication:
- Share your interests and expectations
- Ask questions freely
- Provide feedback during tour
- Communicate any discomfort or concerns

Pacing:
- Guide will match your pace
- Take breaks as needed
- Adjust itinerary if necessary (time permitting)
- Full-day tours include lunch break

Photography:
- Ask permission before photographing locals
- Guide can assist with photo opportunities
- Respect cultural sensitivities

Cultural respect:
- Follow guide's advice on local customs
- Dress modestly in villages and temples
- Remove shoes when entering homes
- Accept offered hospitality politely

SAFETY

Guides prioritize your safety:
- Follow their instructions during activities
- Inform guide of any health concerns
- Carry water and sun protection
- Stay with group in remote areas
- Follow weather advisories

Emergency procedures:
- Guides carry basic first aid
- Guides have emergency contacts
- Guides know local medical facilities
- Call 191 (police) or 195 (ambulance) for emergencies

SPECIAL REQUESTS

Guides can accommodate many special requests:
- Photography-focused tours
- Dietary restrictions (restaurant choices)
- Mobility limitations
- Child-friendly activities
- Specific cultural experiences
- Off-the-beaten-path locations

Communicate special needs when booking.

PROHIBITED ACTIVITIES

Guides will NOT facilitate:
- Illegal drug use or purchase
- Prostitution or sexual services
- Any illegal activities
- Disrespectful behavior toward locals
- Environmental damage
- Trespassing on private property

If guide is asked to facilitate prohibited activities, they will decline and may terminate tour without refund.

DISPUTES & FEEDBACK

If you have concerns:
- Address with guide directly first (most issues can be resolved)
- Contact Vang Vieng Stay support: support@vangviengstay.com
- Provide specific details and timeline
- We will mediate within 48 hours

Leave honest reviews:
- Help other travelers make informed choices
- Help guides improve their service
- Reviews must be respectful and factual
- Posted after completed tour

INSURANCE

RECOMMENDATION: Purchase travel insurance that covers guided activities. Guides are independent contractors, not employees of Vang Vieng Stay.

Verify your insurance covers:
- Guided activities
- Medical evacuation
- Trip cancellation
- Personal liability

BOOKING TIPS

Book in advance during peak season (November - April)
Specify your language needs clearly
Read reviews from previous clients
Communicate special requirements upfront
Confirm meeting details day before
Bring cash for guide payment and expenses
Be flexible with itinerary changes
Enjoy the local experience!

ALTERNATIVES TO PRIVATE GUIDES

If private guide doesn't fit your budget:
- Join group tours (lower per-person cost)
- Use guidebooks and maps for self-guided exploration
- Ask guesthouse staff for recommendations
- Hire guide for specific activities only (climbing, caving)
- Use translation apps for basic communication

Contact support@vangviengstay.com for guide recommendations based on your interests, language needs, and budget.
