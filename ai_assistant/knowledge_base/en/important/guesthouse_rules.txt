VANG VIENG STAY - GUESTHOUSE CHECK-IN/CHECK-OUT RULES & HOUSE RULES

Last Updated: November 2026

Guesthouses in Vang Vieng offer comfortable, affordable accommodation with local hospitality. This guide covers standard policies and house rules.

CHECK-IN PROCEDURES

Standard check-in time: 2:00 PM (14:00)
Early check-in: Subject to availability, may incur fee
Late check-in: Notify guesthouse if arriving after 10:00 PM

Check-in process:
1. Present booking confirmation (email or reference number)
2. Show ID (passport for foreign guests)
3. Pay for stay (cash in LAK or USD)
4. Leave passport or deposit (some guesthouses)
5. Receive room key and orientation
6. Learn about facilities and house rules

Required documents:
- Passport (all foreign guests)
- Booking confirmation
- Payment in full (most guesthouses)

Some guesthouses may require:
- One night's deposit during peak season
- Credit card imprint (rare)
- Signed registration form

EARLY CHECK-IN

If you arrive before 2:00 PM:
- Room may be available (subject to availability)
- Early check-in fee: Usually 50% of nightly rate
- Free early check-in if room was vacant previous night
- Luggage storage available if room not ready

Request early check-in when booking or contact guesthouse directly.

LATE CHECK-IN

If arriving after 10:00 PM:
- Notify guesthouse in advance
- Some guesthouses have 24-hour reception
- Others may lock doors at night
- Arrange key pickup or late check-in procedure
- Additional fee may apply (50,000 - 100,000 LAK)

CHECK-OUT PROCEDURES

Standard check-out time: 12:00 PM (noon)
Late check-out: Subject to availability, may incur fee

Check-out process:
1. Settle any outstanding charges (minibar, damages, etc.)
2. Return room key
3. Room inspection (brief)
4. Receive deposit refund (if applicable)
5. Depart

LATE CHECK-OUT

If you need to stay past noon:
- Request in advance (day before)
- Late check-out until 2:00 PM: Usually 50% of nightly rate
- Late check-out until 6:00 PM: Usually full night's rate
- Subject to availability (depends on next bookings)
- Luggage storage available after check-out

LATE CHECK-OUT FEES

Check-out after 12:00 PM but before 2:00 PM: 50% of nightly rate
Check-out after 2:00 PM but before 6:00 PM: 100% of nightly rate
Check-out after 6:00 PM: Additional night charged

Notify guesthouse if you'll be late to avoid automatic charges.

ROOM TYPES & AMENITIES

Dorm beds:
- Shared room (4-12 beds)
- Shared bathroom
- Lockers (bring your own lock)
- Fan or AC (varies)
- Most affordable option

Single room:
- One bed (single or double)
- Private bathroom (most)
- Fan or AC
- Basic furniture

Double/Twin room:
- One double bed OR two single beds
- Private bathroom
- Fan or AC
- Suitable for couples or friends

Family room:
- Larger space
- Multiple beds
- Private bathroom
- Suitable for families

Standard amenities (most guesthouses):
- Clean bedding and towels
- Wi-Fi (speed varies)
- Hot water (most)
- Basic toiletries (some)
- Air conditioning or fan
- Daily room cleaning

Amenities that vary:
- Breakfast (some include, most don't)
- Swimming pool (some have)
- Restaurant on-site (some have)
- Laundry service (most offer)
- Airport/station transfer (some arrange)
- Tour booking desk (most have)

HOUSE RULES - COMMON POLICIES

Noise and quiet hours:
- Quiet hours: Usually 10:00 PM - 7:00 AM
- Keep noise down in hallways and common areas
- Use headphones for music/videos
- No loud conversations in dorm rooms at night
- Respect other guests' sleep

Smoking:
- Most guesthouses are non-smoking indoors
- Designated smoking areas outside
- Smoking in rooms: Heavy fine (200,000 - 500,000 LAK)
- Vaping: Same rules as smoking

Alcohol and parties:
- No parties in rooms
- No excessive noise
- No bringing large groups to rooms
- Some guesthouses have bars on-site
- Respect quiet hours

Visitors:
- Registered guests only in rooms
- Visitors must register at reception
- No visitors in dorm rooms
- No visitors after 10:00 PM (most guesthouses)
- Some guesthouses don't allow visitors at all

Drugs:
- STRICTLY PROHIBITED
- Lao law imposes severe penalties
- Possession can result in long prison sentences
- Guesthouse will contact police immediately
- No exceptions

Food in rooms:
- Most guesthouses allow snacks and drinks
- No strong-smelling food in dorm rooms
- No cooking in rooms (use designated areas)
- Clean up after yourself

Laundry:
- Laundry service available (most guesthouses)
- Cost: 20,000 - 50,000 LAK per kg
- Return time: Usually 24 hours
- Self-service laundry: Rare

Security:
- Lock your door when leaving
- Use lockers for valuables (bring your own lock)
- Guesthouse not responsible for lost/stolen items
- Safe deposit boxes (some guesthouses)
- Report suspicious activity to reception

PETS

Most guesthouses do NOT allow pets. Exceptions:
- Service animals (with documentation)
- Some guesthouses allow small pets (request in advance)

Check pet policy before booking.

CHILDREN

Family-friendly policies:
- Children welcome at most guesthouses
- Extra bed fee: 50,000 - 150,000 LAK per night
- Children under 2: Usually free (in parent's bed)
- Babysitting: Some guesthouses arrange (additional fee)

Supervise children at all times, especially near pools and roads.

DAMAGES & LOSS

You are responsible for:
- Damage to room or property
- Missing items (towels, remote controls, etc.)
- Stains on bedding or furniture
- Broken fixtures or appliances

Damage charges:
- Assessed at check-out
- Deducted from deposit (if held)
- Charged to credit card (if on file)
- Paid in cash if no deposit

Report accidental damage immediately to avoid misunderstandings.

LOST & FOUND

If you leave items behind:
- Contact guesthouse immediately
- Items held for 30 days (most guesthouses)
- Shipping costs paid by guest
- No responsibility for items not reported promptly

COMPLAINTS & ISSUES

If you have problems during your stay:
1. Report to reception immediately
2. Give guesthouse chance to resolve issue
3. Most issues can be fixed quickly
4. Contact Vang Vieng Stay support if unresolved: support@vangviengstay.com

Common issues and solutions:
- No hot water: Report to reception, usually fixed within hours
- Wi-Fi not working: Restart router or change rooms
- Too noisy: Request room change or earplugs
- AC not working: Report immediately, may change rooms
- Cleanliness concerns: Request fresh cleaning

PAYMENT & DEPOSITS

Payment methods:
- Cash (LAK or USD) - most common
- Credit card - rare, some larger guesthouses accept
- Bank transfer - not common

When payment is due:
- Most guesthouses: At check-in for full stay
- Some: Per night or every few days
- Long stays: Weekly payment may be arranged

Deposit requirements:
- Most guesthouses: No deposit
- Some: Passport as security (returned at check-out)
- Peak season: May require one night's advance payment

Receipts:
- Always request receipt for payment
- Keep for your records
- Needed for any disputes

EXTENDING YOUR STAY

If you want to stay longer:
- Notify reception as early as possible
- Subject to availability
- Same rate if booked in advance
- Rate may change if booking last-minute
- Peak season: Book extension several days ahead

CANCELLATION & MODIFICATION

Cancellation policy varies by guesthouse. Standard policy:
- 7+ days before check-in: 100% refund
- 3-7 days before: 50% refund
- Less than 3 days: No refund
- No-show: No refund

Modification (date changes):
- Allowed with 48+ hours notice
- Subject to availability
- No fee for changes

Contact guesthouse directly or Vang Vieng Stay support for cancellations.

SPECIAL NEEDS & ACCESSIBILITY

Most guesthouses in Vang Vieng are basic and may not accommodate:
- Wheelchair access (many have stairs)
- Elevators (rare)
- Special dietary needs (communicate in advance)
- Medical equipment (limited electricity)

Contact guesthouse before booking to discuss specific needs.

TIPS FOR GUESTHOUSE STAYS

Book in advance during peak season (November - April, Lao New Year)
Read reviews before booking
Confirm amenities important to you (AC, hot water, Wi-Fi)
Bring earplugs for dorm stays
Bring your own lock for lockers
Carry guesthouse business card (for taxi/tuk-tuk drivers)
Respect house rules and other guests
Communicate issues promptly
Enjoy the local hospitality!

CONTACT

For booking assistance: support@vangviengstay.com
Response time: Within 24 hours

For urgent issues during stay: Contact guesthouse reception directly
