VANG VIENG STAY - VISA & ENTRY REQUIREMENTS FOR LAOS

Last Updated: November 2026

Planning your trip to Laos? This guide covers visa requirements, entry procedures, and extensions for visiting Vang Vieng and Laos.

VISA REQUIREMENTS BY NATIONALITY

Visa exemption (no visa required):

ASEAN countries (30 days):
- Thailand, Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia, Philippines
- Cambodia, Vietnam, Myanmar, Brunei
- Just show passport at border

Other exemptions:
- Japan (15 days)
- South Korea (15 days)
- Russia (30 days)
- Switzerland (15 days)
- Luxembourg (15 days)

Visa on arrival (most nationalities):

Available at:
- Wattay International Airport (Vientiane)
- Luang Prabang International Airport
- Pakse International Airport
- Most land border crossings
- Thai-Lao Friendship Bridges

Requirements:
- Passport valid 6+ months
- 2 passport photos (4x6 cm)
- Visa fee in USD (cash, clean bills)
- Completed application form
- Return ticket (sometimes requested)

Visa on arrival fees (approximate):
- Most nationalities: $30 - $50 USD
- US citizens: $50 USD
- Some nationalities: $35 - $45 USD
- Pay in USD only (bring clean, new bills)

Processing time: 15-30 minutes at border

E-Visa (online application):

Available for:
- Tourist visa only
- Select nationalities (check official website)
- Entry at Wattay Airport, Luang Prabang Airport, or Thai-Lao Friendship Bridge 1

Process:
- Apply online 3+ days before travel
- Upload passport photo and scan
- Pay by credit card
- Receive approval letter
- Show letter at border

Cost: $30 - $50 USD + processing fee

Validity: 60 days from issue, 30 days stay

Tourist visa from embassy:

Apply before travel:
- Visit Lao embassy in your country
- Submit application form
- Provide passport and photos
- Pay visa fee
- Wait 3-7 business days

Cost: $30 - $50 USD (varies by embassy)

Validity: 60 days from issue, 30 days stay

VISA TYPES

Tourist visa (T-B3):
- Most common for visitors
- Valid for 30 days
- Single entry
- Can be extended once (see below)
- Cannot work or study

Business visa:
- Required for business activities
- Valid for 30 days
- Can be extended
- Requires sponsor letter
- Different application process

Transit visa:
- For passing through Laos
- Valid 7 days maximum
- Must have onward ticket
- Limited movement

VISA EXTENSIONS

Tourist visa extension:
- Can extend once for additional 30 days
- Total stay maximum: 60 days
- Apply at immigration office before visa expires

Where to extend:
- Vientiane: Department of Immigration
- Luang Prabang: Immigration office
- Other major cities: Immigration offices

Requirements:
- Passport
- Current visa
- 1 passport photo
- Extension fee: $2 per day (approximate)
- Application form

Processing time:
- Standard: 3-5 business days
- Express: 1 day (higher fee)

Overstaying:
- Fine: $10 USD per day
- May face questions at border
- Extended overstay can result in ban
- Always extend before visa expires

ENTRY PROCEDURES

By air:

Arrival process:
1. Fill out arrival/departure card (on plane or at airport)
2. Proceed to immigration
3. Show passport and visa (or get visa on arrival)
4. Pay visa fee if applicable
5. Receive entry stamp
6. Collect luggage
7. Pass through customs
8. Exit airport

By land:

Border crossing process:
1. Exit country of departure
2. Cross border zone
3. Enter Laos immigration
4. Fill out arrival/departure card
5. Show passport and photos
6. Pay visa fee (if visa on arrival)
7. Receive entry stamp
8. Pass through customs
9. Continue to destination

Popular land borders:

Thai-Lao Friendship Bridge 1 (Nong Khai - Vientiane):
- Most popular crossing
- Visa on arrival available
- Bus connections to Vang Vieng
- Open 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM

Thai-Lao Friendship Bridge 4 (Chiang Khong - Huay Xai):
- Gateway to Luang Prabang via boat
- Visa on arrival available
- Beautiful Mekong crossing
- Open 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM

Lao Bao - Dansavanh (Vietnam border):
- Connects Vietnam to Laos
- Visa on arrival available
- Route to Vang Vieng from Vietnam

Boten - Mohan (China border):
- Northern border with China
- Visa on arrival available
- Route from Yunnan province

CUSTOMS REGULATIONS

Allowed items:
- Personal belongings
- 200 cigarettes or 50 cigars
- 1 liter of alcohol
- Reasonable amount of camera equipment
- Laptop and electronics for personal use

Restricted items:
- Drugs (STRICTLY PROHIBITED - severe penalties)
- Weapons and explosives
- Pornographic materials
- Excessive amounts of cash (declare over $2,000 USD)
- Antiques and religious artifacts (without permit)

Declaration required:
- Cash over $2,000 USD
- Professional camera equipment
- Items for commercial use
- Animals or plants

HEALTH REQUIREMENTS

Vaccinations:
- No required vaccinations for most travelers
- Recommended: Hepatitis A & B, Typhoid, Tetanus
- Yellow fever certificate required if coming from endemic area
- Consult travel clinic 4-6 weeks before trip

Malaria:
- Low risk in Vang Vieng
- Higher risk in rural/forest areas
- Discuss prophylaxis with doctor
- Use insect repellent
- Sleep under mosquito net if needed

Dengue fever:
- Present in Laos
- No vaccine widely available
- Prevent mosquito bites
- Use repellent with DEET
- Wear long sleeves at dusk/dawn

Travel insurance:
- Highly recommended
- Coverage for medical emergencies
- Medical evacuation coverage
- Trip cancellation coverage
- Activity coverage (adventure sports)

SPECIAL ENTRY SITUATIONS

Multiple entries:
- Tourist visa is single entry
- Need new visa for re-entry
- Consider multiple-entry visa if planning to leave and return
- Available from embassies (not visa on arrival)

Working in Laos:
- Tourist visa does NOT allow work
- Need work permit and business visa
- Illegal work can result in deportation and ban
- Volunteer work also requires proper visa

Studying in Laos:
- Need education visa
- Sponsorship from educational institution required
- Different application process
- Contact Lao embassy for details

Journalism/filming:
- Special permit required
- Apply through Ministry of Information
- Unauthorized journalism illegal
- Contact embassy before trip

LONG-TERM STAYS

Staying longer than 60 days:
- Tourist visa maximum: 60 days (30 + 30 extension)
- For longer stays, need different visa type
- Business visa can be extended longer
- Consider border runs (exit and re-enter)

Border runs:
- Exit Laos and immediately re-enter
- Get new visa on arrival
- Common practice for long-term travelers
- Not officially encouraged
- May be questioned by immigration

Visa runs to nearby countries:
- Thailand (most popular)
- Vietnam
- Cambodia
- Get new visa on re-entry

EXTENDING YOUR STAY IN VANG VIENG

If you want to stay longer in Vang Vieng:
- Extend visa before it expires
- Plan extension trip to Vientiane or Luang Prabang
- Allow 3-5 days for processing
- Keep copies of all documents
- Maintain valid visa at all times

Immigration offices near Vang Vieng:
- Vientiane (3-4 hours by bus)
- Most travelers extend in Vientiane
- Combine with sightseeing

IMPORTANT TIPS

Before travel:
- Check visa requirements for your nationality
- Ensure passport valid 6+ months
- Bring passport photos
- Bring USD cash for visa fees
- Make copies of important documents
- Purchase travel insurance

At border:
- Be polite and patient
- Have documents ready
- Declare items if required
- Keep visa receipt safe
- Check entry stamp dates

During stay:
- Know your visa expiration date
- Apply for extension well in advance
- Carry passport copy (leave original in safe)
- Respect visa conditions
- Don't overstay

Leaving Laos:
- Show passport and departure card
- Pay any overstay fines
- May be asked about length of stay
- Keep departure stamp proof
