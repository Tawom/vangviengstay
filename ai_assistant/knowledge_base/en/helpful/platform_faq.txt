VANG VIENG STAY - PLATFORM FAQ & HOW IT WORKS

Last Updated: November 2026

Welcome to Vang Vieng Stay! This FAQ answers common questions about our platform, booking process, and policies.

WHAT IS VANG VIENG STAY?

Vang Vieng Stay is an online booking platform that connects travelers with local service providers in Vang Vieng, Laos. We help you book:
- Guesthouses and accommodations
- Tours and activities
- Local guides
- Motorbike rentals
- Buggy car rentals

We are NOT a direct service provider. We connect you with independent, verified local operators.

HOW DOES IT WORK?

Step 1: Browse and choose
- Explore our listings
- Read descriptions and reviews
- Compare prices and options
- Select what you want to book

Step 2: Make a booking request
- Fill out the booking form
- Provide your contact information (email or phone)
- Specify dates and requirements
- Submit your request

Step 3: Wait for confirmation
- Your booking is initially "pending"
- Operator reviews your request
- You receive email confirmation when approved
- Usually confirmed within 24 hours

Step 4: Enjoy your experience
- Meet operator at agreed time/place
- Pay directly to operator (cash)
- Enjoy your tour, stay, or rental
- Leave a review after your experience

DO I NEED TO PAY ONLINE?

No! Vang Vieng Stay operates on a "book now, pay later" model.

- No online payment required
- No credit card needed
- Pay cash directly to operator
- Payment at time of service
- Simple and secure

This means:
- No risk of online payment fraud
- Pay only when you receive service
- Flexible if plans change
- No hidden online fees

IS MY BOOKING GUARANTEED?

Your booking is confirmed when:
1. You submit a booking request
2. Operator approves your request
3. You receive confirmation email

Until you receive confirmation, your booking is "pending" and not guaranteed. Operators may decline bookings for legitimate reasons (availability, safety concerns, etc.).

Always wait for confirmation email before making other plans.

WHAT IF MY BOOKING IS DECLINED?

If an operator declines your booking:
- You'll receive notification via email
- Common reasons: fully booked, unavailable, safety concerns
- No charges or penalties
- You can book alternative options
- Contact support if you need help finding alternatives

We'll help you find alternative options if needed.

HOW DO I CANCEL OR MODIFY?

Cancellations:
- Log into your dashboard
- Find your booking
- Click "Cancel Booking"
- Follow cancellation policy
- Refunds processed per policy

Modifications:
- Contact operator directly (preferred)
- Or contact Vang Vieng Stay support
- Subject to availability
- May incur fees depending on timing

See our detailed Cancellation Policy for specific rules by service type.

WHAT ARE YOUR CANCELLATION POLICIES?

Cancellation policies vary by service type:

Guesthouses:
- 7+ days before: 100% refund
- 3-7 days before: 50% refund
- Less than 3 days: No refund

Tours:
- 48+ hours before: 100% refund
- 24-48 hours before: 50% refund
- Less than 24 hours: No refund

Guides:
- 24+ hours before: 100% refund
- Less than 24 hours: No refund

Motorbikes/Buggies:
- 24+ hours before: 100% refund
- Less than 24 hours: No refund

Full details in our Cancellation Policy document.

DO YOU CHARGE BOOKING FEES?

No! Vang Vieng Stay is free for travelers.

- No booking fees
- No service charges
- No hidden costs
- You pay exactly the listed price
- We earn commission from operators (not from you)

The price you see is the price you pay.
