VANG VIENG STAY - RESTAURANT & FOOD RECOMMENDATIONS

Last Updated: November 2026

Vang Vieng offers diverse dining options from authentic Lao street food to international cuisine. This guide helps you find the best places to eat.

LAO FOOD ESSENTIALS

Must-try Lao dishes:

Larb (Laab):
- National dish of Laos
- Minced meat (pork, chicken, beef, duck, or fish)
- Mixed with herbs, lime, chili, toasted rice powder
- Served with sticky rice and fresh vegetables
- Can be spicy (specify mild if preferred)
- Price: 30,000 - 60,000 LAK

Tam Mak Hung (Papaya Salad):
- Shredded green papaya
- Pounded with chili, lime, fish sauce, tomatoes
- Fresh, spicy, sour, and savory
- Often served with sticky rice
- Price: 20,000 - 40,000 LAK

Khao Piak Sen:
- Lao noodle soup
- Handmade rice noodles in pork or chicken broth
- Topped with herbs, bean sprouts, fried garlic
- Comfort food, great for breakfast
- Price: 20,000 - 35,000 LAK

Khao Jii Sandwich:
- French-influenced baguette sandwich
- Filled with pâté, ham, vegetables, chili sauce
- Popular street food
- Price: 10,000 - 20,000 LAK

Sai Oua (Lao Sausage):
- Grilled pork sausage with herbs
- Served with sticky rice and fresh vegetables
- Often eaten as snack or with drinks
- Price: 20,000 - 40,000 LAK

Or Lam:
- Traditional Luang Prabang stew
- Mixed vegetables, meat, and dill weed
- Thick, hearty, flavorful
- Price: 40,000 - 70,000 LAK

Sticky Rice (Khao Niew):
- Staple food of Laos
- Steamed glutinous rice
- Eaten with hands (right hand only)
- Served with most meals
- Price: 5,000 - 10,000 LAK per basket

WHERE TO EAT

Night Market:
Location: Main road, center of town
Hours: 5:00 PM - 10:00 PM daily
Price: 15,000 - 40,000 LAK per dish
Atmosphere: Casual, outdoor seating, lively

Best for:
- Authentic Lao street food
- Budget meals
- Local atmosphere
- Trying multiple dishes

Recommended stalls:
- Look for busy stalls (high turnover = fresh food)
- Papaya salad stalls
- Grilled meat skewers
- Noodle soup vendors
- Fresh fruit shakes

Morning Market:
Location: Near bus station
Hours: 6:00 AM - 11:00 AM
Price: 10,000 - 30,000 LAK
Atmosphere: Local market, authentic experience

Best for:
- Early breakfast
- Local food experience
- Fresh ingredients
- Observing daily life

Look for:
- Noodle soup vendors
- Sticky rice and grilled meat
- Fresh fruit
- Local coffee

Guesthouse Restaurants:
Most guesthouses have restaurants serving:
- Lao dishes
- Western food (pizza, pasta, burgers)
- Breakfast options
- Price: 30,000 - 80,000 LAK per meal
- Convenient but may lack authenticity

Town Restaurants:
Concentrated along main road and riverside
Price range: 30,000 - 120,000 LAK per meal
Wide variety of cuisines
Good for dinner and special occasions

RIVERSIDE DINING

Popular riverside restaurants:
- Beautiful views of Nam Song River
- Relaxing atmosphere
- Sunset dining
- Slightly higher prices
- Price: 50,000 - 150,000 LAK per meal

Best time to visit:
- Late afternoon for sunset
- Early evening for dinner
- Avoid rainy season afternoons

INTERNATIONAL FOOD OPTIONS

Western food:
- Pizza, pasta, burgers available
- Quality varies widely
- Price: 40,000 - 100,000 LAK
- Best at established restaurants

Thai food:
- Similar to Lao but different flavors
- Widely available
- Price: 30,000 - 70,000 LAK
- Good alternative if Lao food too spicy

Vietnamese food:
- Widely available due to proximity and shared culinary influences
- Popular dishes include Pho (noodle soup), Banh Mi (baguette sandwiches), and fresh spring rolls
- Light, fresh, and herb-heavy
- Price: 25,000 - 50,000 LAK
- Great alternative for a lighter meal

Indian food:
- Several Indian restaurants in town
- Curry, naan, tandoori
- Price: 40,000 - 80,000 LAK
- Vegetarian-friendly

Chinese food:
- Available at some restaurants
- Stir-fries, noodles, rice dishes
- Price: 30,000 - 60,000 LAK

Vegetarian/Vegan options:
- Many restaurants offer vegetarian dishes
- Lao cuisine uses fish sauce frequently (specify no fish sauce)
- Tofu widely available
- Fresh vegetables abundant
- Price: 25,000 - 60,000 LAK

CAFES & COFFEE

Lao coffee:
- Strong, sweet, often with condensed milk
- Grown in southern Laos (Bolaven Plateau)
- Available at most cafes
- Price: 15,000 - 30,000 LAK

Western-style coffee:
- Espresso, cappuccino, latte available
- Quality varies
- Price: 20,000 - 45,000 LAK
- Best at expat-owned cafes

Popular cafes:
- Good for breakfast
- Wi-Fi available
- Work-friendly
- Relaxing atmosphere
- Price: 30,000 - 70,000 LAK for coffee + pastry

DRINKS

Local beers:
- Beerlao: National beer, excellent quality
- Available everywhere
- Price: 15,000 - 25,000 LAK per bottle
- Try Beerlao Dark or Beerlao Light

Fresh fruit shakes:
- Made with fresh fruit, ice, sugar
- Mango, pineapple, watermelon, coconut
- Price: 15,000 - 25,000 LAK
- Refreshing in hot weather

Lao Lao (rice whiskey):
- Traditional spirit
- Strong (40% alcohol)
- Often offered as hospitality
- Price: 20,000 - 40,000 LAK per small bottle

Fresh coconut:
- Available at markets and street vendors
- Price: 10,000 - 20,000 LAK
- Refreshing and healthy

FOOD SAFETY TIPS

Choose restaurants wisely:
- Eat at busy places (high turnover = fresh food)
- Look for clean kitchens
- Observe food handling
- Trust your instincts

Water safety:
- Drink only bottled or filtered water
- Avoid ice in small establishments
- Bottled water: 5,000 - 10,000 LAK per liter
- Many guesthouses provide filtered water

Food handling:
- Eat food that's cooked fresh and hot
- Avoid raw or undercooked meat
- Peel fruits yourself
- Be cautious with raw vegetables
- Wash hands before eating

Street food safety:
- Choose stalls with many local customers
- Watch food being prepared
- Eat food that's cooked to order
- Avoid food sitting out for long periods

DIETARY RESTRICTIONS

Vegetarian/Vegan:
- Specify "no meat, no fish sauce"
- Tofu dishes widely available
- Fresh vegetables abundant
- Communicate clearly

Gluten-free:
- Rice-based dishes naturally gluten-free
- Avoid soy sauce (contains wheat)
- Communicate restrictions clearly
- Sticky rice is safe

Nut allergies:
- Peanuts commonly used in Lao cooking
- Specify allergy clearly
- Avoid larb and some salads
- Ask about ingredients

Halal/Kosher:
- Limited options
- Communicate restrictions
- Vegetarian options safest
- Bring backup snacks

TIPS FOR ENJOYING LAO FOOD

Be adventurous:
- Try new dishes
- Ask locals for recommendations
- Don't be afraid of spices
- Embrace different textures

Spice levels:
- Lao food can be very spicy
- Ask for "mild" or "no spicy" if needed
- Start with small amounts
- Build up tolerance gradually

Eating with locals:
- Accept invitations to share meals
- Try everything offered
- Show appreciation
- Learn dining etiquette

Learning food names:
- Ask about dishes
- Learn Lao food names
- Helps with ordering
- Shows respect for culture
