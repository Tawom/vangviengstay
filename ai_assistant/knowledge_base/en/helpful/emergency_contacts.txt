EMERGENCY NUMBERS - LAOS

National emergency numbers:
- Police: 191
- Ambulance: 195
- Fire: 190
- Tourist Police: Contact through guesthouse or local police

These numbers work from any phone in Laos (mobile or landline).

TRAVEL INSURANCE

ESSENTIAL for all travelers to Laos.

Before your trip:
- Purchase comprehensive travel insurance
- Verify coverage includes:
  * Medical emergencies
  * Medical evacuation
  * Trip cancellation
  * Lost/stolen belongings
  * Adventure activities (if applicable)
  * Motorbike rental (if riding)

During emergency:
1. Contact insurance provider immediately
2. Get reference number
3. Follow their instructions
4. Keep all receipts and documentation
5. Get police report if required

Popular insurance providers:

- Claim procedure

MEDICAL EMERGENCIES

For medical emergencies:

Step 1: Assess the situation
- Is it life-threatening? Call 195 (ambulance)
- Is it urgent but not life-threatening? Go to hospital or clinic
- Is it minor? Visit pharmacy or clinic

Step 2: Get medical care
- Vang Vieng Hospital for basic care
- Private clinic for better facilities
- Guesthouse can arrange transport

Step 3: Contact insurance
- Call insurance emergency number
- Get authorization for treatment
- Understand coverage and procedures

Step 4: Documentation
- Keep all medical reports
- Save receipts for all payments
- Get police report if accident-related
- Document everything with photos

Step 5: Follow-up
- Contact insurance for claims
- Keep copies of all documents
- Follow medical advice
- Rest and recover

Common medical issues:

Traveler's diarrhea:
- Stay hydrated
- Eat at busy restaurants
- Drink bottled water only
- Visit pharmacy for medication
- See doctor if severe or persistent

Heat exhaustion:
- Move to cool place
- Drink water with electrolytes
- Rest in shade
- Cool down with wet cloths
- Seek medical help if symptoms worsen

Mosquito-borne illness (dengue):
- Symptoms: High fever, severe headache, joint pain
- Seek medical attention immediately
- No specific treatment, supportive care only
- Prevent with repellent and long sleeves

Motorbike accidents:
- Most common serious injury for tourists
- Call 195 (ambulance) or get to hospital
- Contact insurance immediately
- Get police report
- Document injuries and scene

Dehydration:
- Common in hot weather
- Drink 3-4 liters water per day
- Use electrolyte packets
- Rest in shade
- Seek help if dizzy or confused

Food poisoning:
- Nausea, vomiting, diarrhea
- Stay hydrated
- Rest
- See doctor if severe
- Avoid solid foods initially

POLICE & LEGAL ISSUES

Contacting police:
- Emergency: 191
- Non-emergency: Through guesthouse
- Tourist police: Available in major tourist areas

When to contact police:
- Accident (especially with injuries)
- Theft or robbery
- Assault or harassment
- Scams or fraud
- Lost passport
- Any legal issue

At police station:
- Bring passport
- Bring guesthouse staff if possible (translation)
- Stay calm and polite
- Get copy of police report
- Contact embassy if serious issue
- Contact insurance if applicable

Lost passport:
1. Report to police immediately
2. Get police report
3. Contact your embassy
4. Apply for emergency travel document
5. Allow 1-3 days for processing
6. Bring passport photos and copies

Theft or robbery:
1. Report to police immediately
2. Get police report (needed for insurance)
3. Cancel stolen credit cards
4. Contact embassy if passport stolen
5. Contact insurance for claims
6. Replace essential items

Accidents:
1. Ensure everyone's safety
2. Call 195 (ambulance) if injured
3. Call 191 (police) for report
4. Don't admit fault or argue
5. Document scene with photos
6. Get witness information
7. Contact insurance
8. Contact embassy if serious

SAFETY PREVENTION

General safety tips:
- Use common sense
- Don't flash expensive items
- Secure valuables in guesthouse safe
- Lock doors and windows
- Use lockers for belongings
- Stay aware of surroundings
- Avoid walking alone at night
- Stick to well-lit areas

Motorbike safety:
- Always wear helmet
- Valid license required
- Don't ride under influence
- Check bike before riding
- Ride defensively
- Avoid night riding
- Know your limits

Water safety:
- Wear life jacket when tubing
- Don't tube under influence
- Know how to swim
- Tube with others
- Stay away from strong currents
- Don't jump from bridges

Activity safety:
- Follow guide instructions
- Use provided safety equipment
- Know your physical limits
- Don't participate under influence
- Check weather conditions
- Inform someone of your plans

Health safety:
- Drink bottled water only
- Use insect repellent
- Wash hands frequently
- Eat at busy restaurants
- Stay hydrated
- Use sun protection
- Get travel vaccinations

NATURAL DISASTERS & WEATHER

Flooding:
- Common during rainy season (June-October)
- Can affect roads and activities
- Follow local advice
- Avoid flooded areas
- Contact guesthouse for updates

Storms:
- Heavy rain and thunderstorms common
- Seek shelter indoors
- Stay away from rivers
- Avoid outdoor activities
- Wait for storm to pass

Landslides:
- Possible in hilly areas after heavy rain
- Avoid unstable slopes
- Follow local warnings
- Don't travel on affected roads

Earthquakes:
- Rare in Laos
- If occurs, move to open area
- Stay away from buildings
- Follow local guidance
